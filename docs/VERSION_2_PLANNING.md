# Modern Chain Link Company - Version 2.0 Feature Roadmap & Backlog

This document outlines the planned feature backlog and architectural expansions for **Version 2.0** of Modern Chain Link Company.

---

## 1. Feature Backlog Overview

| Feature Module | Priority | Target Version | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Inventory Management** | High | v2.1 | Track raw GI wire stock, mesh rolls in stock, barbed wire reels, cement poles, and binding wire quantities with low-stock alerts. |
| **Supplier Management** | Medium | v2.2 | Maintain supplier profiles (TATA, Micon, Local vendors), raw material purchase orders, and vendor payments. |
| **Employee & Labour Management** | Medium | v2.2 | Track fitting team schedules, daily wages, labour payout ledgers, and site assignment histories. |
| **Customer Self-Service Portal** | High | v2.3 | Allow customers to check order fulfillment status, download PDF invoices, and view payment balances via phone number login. |
| **Advanced Business Analytics** | Medium | v2.4 | Visual charts for monthly revenue trends, material sales distribution (Fence vs Barbed Wire), and profit margin analytics. |
| **Multi-Branch Support** | Low | v3.0 | Multi-warehouse and multi-office location support with role-based access control (RBAC). |

---
